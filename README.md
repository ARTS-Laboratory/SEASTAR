# SEASTAR

SEASTAR (Scalable Electric Architecture for Shipboard Testing, Analysis, and Research) is a family of generic vessel models for electric-ship research. The models provide consistent hull geometries and baseline vessel characteristics for studying shipboard power systems, propulsion, energy storage, controls, and other integrated electric-vessel technologies.

Individual designs are identified by their nominal vessel length, such as SEASTAR-8, SEASTAR-12, and SEASTAR-20.
